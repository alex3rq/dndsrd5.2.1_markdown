## Class Details

### Barbarian — [01_Barbarian.md](01_Barbarian.md)
Rage-fueled warrior. Features: Rage, Unarmored Defense, Weapon Mastery, Reckless Attack, Danger Sense, Extra Attack, Brutal Strike, Relentless Rage, Primal Champion.
**Subclass:** Path of the Berserker — Frenzy, Mindless Rage, Retaliation, Intimidating Presence.

### Bard — [02_Bard.md](02_Bard.md)
Musical spellcaster and skill expert. Features: Bardic Inspiration, Spellcasting (full caster), Expertise, Jack of All Trades, Font of Inspiration, Countercharm, Magical Secrets, Words of Creation.
**Subclass:** College of Lore — Bonus Proficiencies, Cutting Words, Magical Discoveries, Peerless Skill.

### Cleric — [03_Cleric.md](03_Cleric.md)
Divine spellcaster and healer. Features: Spellcasting (full caster), Divine Order, Channel Divinity, Sear Undead, Blessed Strikes, Divine Intervention.
**Subclass:** Life Domain — Disciple of Life, Preserve Life, Blessed Healer, Supreme Healing.

### Druid — [04_Druid.md](04_Druid.md)
Nature spellcaster and shapeshifter. Features: Spellcasting (full caster), Druidic, Primal Order, Wild Shape, Wild Companion, Wild Resurgence, Elemental Fury, Beast Spells, Archdruid.
**Subclass:** Circle of the Land — Circle Spells, Land's Aid, Natural Recovery, Nature's Ward, Nature's Sanctuary.

### Fighter — [05_Fighter.md](05_Fighter.md)
Master of weapons and combat. Features: Fighting Style, Second Wind, Weapon Mastery, Action Surge, Tactical Mind, Extra Attack (×2, ×3), Indomitable, Tactical Master.
**Subclass:** Champion — Improved Critical, Remarkable Athlete, Additional Fighting Style, Heroic Warrior, Superior Critical, Survivor.

### Monk — [06_Monk.md](06_Monk.md)
Unarmed combatant with ki focus. Features: Martial Arts, Unarmored Defense, Monk's Focus, Unarmored Movement, Deflect Attacks, Stunning Strike, Evasion, Acrobatic Movement, Perfect Focus.
**Subclass:** Warrior of the Open Hand — Open Hand Technique, Wholeness of Body, Fleet Step, Quivering Palm.

### Paladin — [07_Paladin.md](07_Paladin.md)
Holy warrior and protector. Features: Lay on Hands, Spellcasting (half caster), Weapon Mastery, Fighting Style, Paladin's Smite, Channel Divinity, Aura of Protection, Radiant Strikes.
**Subclass:** Oath of Devotion — Sacred Weapon, Aura of Devotion, Smite of Protection, Holy Nimbus.

### Ranger — [08_Ranger.md](08_Ranger.md)
Survivalist and tracker. Features: Spellcasting (half caster), Favored Enemy, Weapon Mastery, Deft Explorer, Fighting Style, Extra Attack, Roving, Tireless, Nature's Veil, Foe Slayer.
**Subclass:** Hunter — Hunter's Lore, Hunter's Prey, Defensive Tactics, Superior Hunter's Prey, Superior Hunter's Defense.

### Rogue — [09_Rogue.md](09_Rogue.md)
Stealth expert and striker. Features: Expertise, Sneak Attack, Thieves' Cant, Weapon Mastery, Cunning Action, Steady Aim, Cunning Strike, Uncanny Dodge, Evasion, Reliable Talent, Elusive, Stroke of Luck.
**Subclass:** Thief — Fast Hands, Second-Story Work, Supreme Sneak, Use Magic Device.

### Sorcerer — [10_Sorcerer.md](10_Sorcerer.md)
Innate spellcaster with metamagic. Features: Spellcasting (full caster), Innate Sorcery, Font of Magic, Metamagic, Sorcerous Restoration, Sorcery Incarnate, Arcane Apotheosis.
**Subclass:** Draconic Sorcery — Draconic Resilience, Elemental Affinity, Dragon Wings, Dragon Companion.

### Warlock — [11_Warlock.md](11_Warlock.md)
Pact-based spellcaster with invocations. Features: Eldritch Invocations, Pact Magic, Magical Cunning, Contact Patron, Mystic Arcanum, Eldritch Master. Includes 25+ Eldritch Invocations.
**Subclass:** Fiend Patron — Dark One's Blessing, Dark One's Own Luck, Fiendish Resilience, Hurl Through Hell.

### Wizard — [12_Wizard.md](12_Wizard.md)
Scholarly arcane spellcaster. Features: Spellcasting (full caster), Ritual Adept, Arcane Recovery, Scholar, Memorize Spell, Spell Mastery, Signature Spells. Includes full Wizard spell list by level.
**Subclass:** Evoker — Evocation Savant, Potent Cantrip, Sculpt Spells, Empowered Evocation, Overchannel.
